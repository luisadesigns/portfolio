Description

I’m a recent graduate in Digital Game Development with a focus on Game Design. I’m particularly interested in designing gameplay mechanics and creating engaging player experiences through systems, progression, and meaningful player interactions. During my studies and internship, I worked on serious games, prototyping, playtesting, and modular game design, gaining experience in designing and documenting game mechanics from concept to implementation.

I originally became interested in game design through my passion for games and the way mechanics can shape how players experience a game. I enjoy experimenting with ideas, figuring out how different mechanics interact, and iterating on them through testing and feedback. I’m currently looking to gain more industry experience, build my portfolio, and continue developing my skills while contributing to interesting game projects.

PROGRAM BRIEFING
The simulation has 4 levels of increasing complexity, each with a new mechanic.
Level 1 — Initial Support: recognize priorities and respond to simple tickets.
Level 2 — Priority Management: multiple simultaneous tickets with different urgencies.
Level 3 — Operational Trade-offs: escalations become a limited resource.
Level 4 — Operational Crisis: an unexpected event with multiple customers affected by the same cause.
Feedback on each decision is shown at the end of the level: what happened, why, and how to improve.

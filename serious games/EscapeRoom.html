Incident response procedures manual. Defines the classic phishing compromise chain.
Chapter 4 · Phishing Attack Chain
Most email compromises follow the same sequence:
1. Receipt of the malicious email
2. User interaction (click, download, opening attachment)
3. Payload execution (usually an executable)
4. Establishing communication with the command-and-control server
5. Lateral movement or exfiltration

Reconstructing this chain is essential to identify the entry vector and block future attempts.
Cross-reference this chain with the history in the Digital Archive to confirm IOC #2.
